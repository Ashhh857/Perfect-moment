Portfolio tips

  • Upload only your own genuine project photos.
  • Choose well-lit images with the decoration clearly visible.
  • Avoid private customer information in the frame.
  • Use specific titles such as “Birthday Decoration — Gomti Nagar”.
  • Keep the best 20–40 photos visible rather than uploading everything.
